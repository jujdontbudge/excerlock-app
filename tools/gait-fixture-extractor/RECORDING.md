# Record the treadmill clips

Use the stock iPhone Camera app: front camera, Video, 1080p/30. Put the phone where it sits on your treadmill. Keep the original videos; do not trim, stabilize, crop or send through a messaging app. Each take lasts 25–30 seconds. Start the stated action before recording, except the two start/stop takes.

Record three separate sessions, preferably on different days or with a fresh setup each time. There are 20 takes per session: six walking, twelve not walking and two start/stop. Session three is held out: keep it unopened until settings are frozen using sessions one and two. Use your supported speeds, ordinary lighting, and the same supported camera position. "Wide" means include as much lower body as this setup permits. A helper should confirm what your feet do, especially for the side rails and bobbing takes. No one needs to upload footage.

Rename each original file exactly as below (use `.mp4` instead if that is its original extension).

## AirDrop and judge the clips

Select the original videos in Photos → Share → AirDrop → your Mac. Put all renamed files in a local folder such as `~/Movies/treadmill-clips`. Keep recordings outside the repository. Install dependencies with `npm ci` in this checkout if needed.

First judge only sessions one and two. The tool leaves session-three files unopened:

```sh
EXCER_SIM=ExcerLock-treadmill EXCER_METRO_PORT=8092 ./excer gait eval ~/Movies/treadmill-clips
```

After the detector settings in `tools/gait-eval/frozen-settings.json` are frozen, judge all 60, with session three held out:

```sh
EXCER_SIM=ExcerLock-treadmill EXCER_METRO_PORT=8092 ./excer gait eval ~/Movies/treadmill-clips --held-out session-three
```

Open the printed local `report.md`. It compares old and new scans, reports the pass time or rejection reason for every clip, and prints GO, NO-GO or PENDING. GO needs zero incidental passes in session three and at least 17 of all 18 walking takes within 10 seconds. Deliberate bobbing is shown separately. Missing clips mean PENDING. If settings change after viewing session three, record a new held-out session before another release decision.

Build 17 has a native expiry defect (#164): the Stage 1 JS fix cannot establish suspended re-lock until the next binary. Do not release it on a JS-only expiry claim. On build 18 try standing at the odd angle for 60 seconds, walking, background/cancel/resume, and the full 15-minute deadline with ExcerLock suspended. Confirm the warning and real Screen Time re-lock; note any lag. Build 18 additionally needs live checks of background walkers, running-belt side rails, knees and console shake. Video replay cannot prove these live device behaviours.

For the build 18 camera checks, repeat the same evaluation commands with `--payload build18`. It uses the production analyzer and reports processing timings. The default evaluates build 17 payloads. Keep the third session unopened for both until thresholds are frozen; changing thresholds after either final evaluation needs a fresh held-out session.
