# Round 2 style — "Unrot" cartoon set (captain's direction, 2026-09-17)

Reference: inspiration/refs/unrot-earn-your-screen-time-*.webp.

- No realistic phones: the screen is a white rounded slab (~120px radius), no bezel/island/status bar/home bar, bleeding off the frame edge. Stacked slabs offset like paper when two are needed.
- Flat pastel canvas over the full frame (ours: orange #F2792C → peach), one flat-vector illustrated scene in the bottom third (walking path / park, warm tones).
- White pill/capsule cards floating in space: icon · label · value, soft shadow, staggered offsets and 1–3° rotations.
- Headline: heavy rounded geometric sans, white, 2 lines, centered, top ~15%. No badge, no subline unless needed.
- Mascot (optional): cute cartoon character, hero only; tiny icon elsewhere.
- Laurels + stats: thin white line-art wreaths under the hero headline.
- Real app icons in a loose grid cropped by the slab edge.
- Before/after: two cards, fat fully-rounded bars (red → green/orange), big metric + glyph, "Daily average" caption, no axes.
- Feature tiles: 2×3 white rounded squares, label on top, one big flat icon.
- Everything rounded (≥24px), white-on-pastel, soft shadows, zero thin strokes, no black outlines (deliberate departure from design/UI-STYLE.md for marketing art).
- Text: white on canvas, near-black on cards, one accent colour for meaning only. No orange body text.
