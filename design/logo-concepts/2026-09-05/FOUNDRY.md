## Concept 1

Initial files: concept-1-opaque.png, concept-1-normalized.png, concept-1-glyph.png, concept-1.png. Rejected: black background and distressed artifacts.

Prompt (B2):

```text
Use case: new-asset-with-style-reference
Asset type: 1024 × 1024 ExcerLock iOS icon: MOVING LOCK — CONCEPT 1.
Primary request: Create one single centered chunky rounded closed padlock with a thick shackle and solid body, with a very simple forward-striding human pictogram cut out of the body as negative space in place of the keyhole, a circular head and bold bent limbs, forming one unified lock-and-movement symbol. The subject must read instantly at 60 pt display size.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat, uniform solid magenta #FF00FF filling every background pixel to every corner, solely for chroma-key removal. No gradient, texture, shadow, glow, vignette, floor, reflection, particles, or lighting variation in the background.
Style/medium: flat 2D vector-like bitmap glyph; pure white #FFFFFF strokes and fills; rounded stroke ends and joins, generous stroke weight; simple friendly iOS-glyph geometry; zero gradients, zero glow, zero 3D, zero outline-only wire art — solid confident shapes.
Color palette: pure white #FFFFFF only in the subject. Do not use cyan, blue, teal, pink, gold, red, gray, black, or magenta in the subject unless listed above.
Composition/framing: one centered subject occupying roughly 65% of the canvas with generous even padding; every subject pixel well separated from all four edges; strong readable silhouette at 60 pt.
Constraints: the subject must be opaque and confidently solid-filled, not hollow wire art and not merely thickened outlines. Crisp clean outer edge for chroma keying. No outer halo, bloom, cast shadow, energy field, black box, text, UI, frame, face, character, logo, or watermark. One subject only.
```

Correction reference: concept-1-opaque.png

```text
Precise object edit. Preserve the exact concept and overall silhouette in the reference image, but clean it into a pristine flat two-color graphic. Pure solid white #FFFFFF symbol on perfectly uniform solid magenta #FF00FF background. Replace ALL black background, ALL black holes, ALL black negative spaces with magenta. Remove ALL distressed speckles, grit, texture, gray shading and stray pixels. Smooth crisp vector-like edges. The running-person cutout must be completely solid magenta, a simple clear circular head and running body cut out of the white padlock. No shadow, no 3D, no black anywhere. Square canvas.
```
