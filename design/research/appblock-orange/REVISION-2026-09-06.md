# AppBlock visual correction — September 6

## Evidence inspected

- [Blocking home](https://screensdesign.com/apps/appblock-apps-web-blocker/?vs=293778): compact status header, primary action group, schedule cards with freestanding clock emoji, target illustration, four-tab navigation.
- [Blocking types](https://screensdesign.com/apps/appblock-apps-web-blocker/?vs=293807): rounded utility cards, clear title/subtitle hierarchy, compact symbolic illustrations.
- [Weekly Insights](https://screensdesign.com/apps/appblock-apps-web-blocker/?vs=293821): segmented period control, date navigation, large summary metric, chart, explanatory sections.
- [Profile](https://screensdesign.com/apps/appblock-apps-web-blocker/?vs=293857): grouped rows, inset separators, native line icons, restrained headings. Its promotions and app-icon customization are not copied into ExcerLock.

## Correction to the earlier design direction

The earlier prompts confused brand color with illustration palette. AppBlock's clock/target artwork uses recognizable object colors and dimensional emoji rendering. ExcerLock controls stay orange; objects may be silver, gold, blue, green, purple, or natural skin/clothing colors. Decorative peach icon plates are removed. Functional option outlines, inputs, segmented controls and grouped utility cards remain.

Use white as the dominant surface, faint orange at top only. Preserve native sans-serif hierarchy. Utility/navigation icons remain simple rather than oversized emoji. The current main-tab concepts use the user-requested labels Home, Conditions, Analytics, Settings; this is a design revision, not a silent edit of the product implementation.

## Function mapping

- Home: blocking state, earned/remaining time, today's condition progress, locked-app access. No workout plans or manual claim CTA.
- Conditions: configured event/state rules, enable toggles, add/edit actions, locked apps, schedules and rules.
- Analytics: reclaimed time, spend/earn comparison, exercise activity, streak, earning breakdown. No per-app usage breakdown.
- Settings: permission health, account, preferences, subscription and support. Additional lower settings remain in the PRD; this is a first-viewport concept.

The main examples depict an existing user's configured state, not pre-created defaults. Data and charts are illustrative, not measurements. Raster concepts still require exact token, navigation-glyph and data binding normalization during implementation. Subscription prices and offers remain unconfigured. No app code or live purchases were changed.
