## Acceptance rules and known limits

**The rebuild should let a verified purchaser use the app immediately and offer sign-in to save/recover their account.** Local Conditions do not require forced post-purchase registration. This is a proposed product change: the reference still requires explicit sign-in and independent API admission after purchase ([ADR 0087](/tmp/excer-ref/docs/adr/0087-recoverable-subscription-verification.md)). Keeping that rule means a brand-new payer can still be blocked during an API/auth outage. Existing remembered admission protects returning users, not that first purchase. A webhook is reconciliation evidence; its absence must not turn a verified purchase into “unpaid.” Adapty documents iOS local store verification when its backend is unavailable. This cannot bypass authentication for private server data. [Adapty local access levels](https://adapty.io/docs/local-access-levels).

For every row: retain answers, staged Conditions, selected plan and any uncertain purchase; never automatically submit another purchase; always provide a usable Back/Retry/Restore/help route. A confirmed expiration or revocation may end premium, but must leave account deletion, export, subscription management and help reachable. “Unknown,” offline, cancellation of auto-renewal, and an empty restore are different states.

**Reinstall limit:** StoreKit restores purchases, not quiz answers or local Conditions. An anonymous draft deleted with the app cannot be guaranteed recoverable without a surviving backup or an authenticated cloud draft. The rebuild needs optional “Save my progress” before reinstall, and real draft sync for signed-in users. Never promise universal reinstall continuity based on Keychain survival. Supabase explicitly distinguishes anonymous users from recoverable permanent accounts. [Anonymous sign-ins](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-anonymous), [purchase restore](https://adapty.io/docs/react-native-restore-purchase).

## How to run the phone tests

- Use a signed development build or TestFlight on a real iPhone, never Expo Go. For vendor integration proof, use Apple's real Sandbox store with the Xcode scheme's local `.storekit` configuration **disabled**. Local StoreKit fixtures do not prove the ASC catalog, Adapty ownership or webhook path.
- **Base sandbox setting S0:** an existing Sandbox Apple Account under Settings → Developer → Sandbox Apple Account/Account → Manage. Developer Mode is needed for development builds. Keep the normal iCloud account for Sign in with Apple. Development-signed purchases do not require signing out of iCloud. For TestFlight sandbox controls Apple also describes signing out of production **Media & Purchases** and selecting the sandbox account in Developer settings; verify the displayed sandbox identity/environment on the actual OS. Do not sign out of iCloud just to change the purchasing account. [Apple sandbox guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/testing-in-app-purchases-with-sandbox).
- Prepare existing testers A (intro eligible), B (intro consumed), C (no purchases), two app accounts, and a test email inbox. Reinstall does not reset introductory eligibility. Account-history clearing can take several minutes; prepare A before a timed run. Only future testers should change device sandbox controls; no settings were changed for this research.
- **S3** means device sandbox renewal rate “3 minutes”: monthly renews in about 3 minutes, annual in about 36 minutes; billing retry is about 6 minutes and grace about 3 minutes at this rate. These are sandbox estimates plus delivery lag, not production durations. For ordinary recovery tests use a slower existing rate so the trial does not expire during sign-in. [Apple rate table and interrupted-purchase controls](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-in-app-purchases/manage-sandbox-apple-account-settings/).
- Each row states incremental hands-on/wall time after its starting state, extra sandbox controls, reinstall (**R**) and second phone (**D2**). “No” means neither is required. Outage cases need a **preconfigured device-local network blocker** that drops only the named service, with no TLS decryption or credential logging. Fault fixtures need an already prepared QA build; they are not capabilities established by this research and must not alter shared services.
- Read evidence using existing PostHog/Sentry views or read/list/get APIs. Record build, runtime, OTA, OS, sandbox lane and timestamps in the QA record; do not capture receipts, OTPs, JWTs, email, Apple identifiers, health/location/Screen Time selections or onboarding answers.

## Event contract used in the tables

`PH` = consented PostHog capture; `SE` = Sentry technical diagnostic. Normal cancellation, denial, no purchases and pending approval are **not exceptions**. “Must fire” means invoke/enqueue when permitted, then verify ingestion after reconnect; analytics opt-out, OS termination and bounded queue overflow prevent an absolute delivery guarantee. No analytics/Sentry call may be awaited by checkout, auth or boot. PostHog documents an offline queue; test the installed 4.61 behavior instead of assuming every option in current docs exists. [PostHog React Native](https://posthog.com/docs/libraries/react-native).

| Shorthand | Exact events and assertions |
|---|---|
| **C(op,outcome)** — existing | `checkout_attempt_started`, `checkout_stage_recorded`, `checkout_attempt_completed`; `operation=purchase/restore`, random process-local `attempt_id`, placement, actual `plan_id` (`none` for restore), stage/results, timing, bounded numeric `failure_code`, `failure_kind`, `entitlement_result`. Terminal outcomes: `success`, `cancelled`, `pending`, `already_subscribed`, `not_found`, `failed`. One terminal per settled accepted call; no terminal fabricated after a kill and no new attempt for a rejected double tap. SDK success remains success even if admission subsequently fails. `sheet_observation=inactive` is only an AppState proxy; visually verify the sheet. [ADR 0090](/tmp/excer-ref/docs/adr/0090-client-checkout-attempt-diagnostics.md). |
| **W** — existing + proposed | Existing `paywall_viewed` means products loaded, not merely mounted; `paywall_error{operation}`. Proposed `paywall_presented`, `paywall_load_result{outcome,source,missing_product_count}`, `paywall_closed{destination}` distinguish missing prices, actionable checkout and exits. |
| **E** — proposed | `entitlement_checked{result=active/trial/grace/expired/revoked/unknown,source=store/cache/server,environment}` and `access_decided{decision=allow/recover/deny,reason_code}`; no raw transaction/identity. Record valid local access separately from API admission. |
| **A** — existing + proposed | Existing `sign_in_failed{provider,surface,reason_class,failure_code}`. Proposed `sign_in_started`, `sign_in_completed{provider,outcome=success/cancelled/failed}`, `account_connection_result{outcome,reason_code}`, `sign_out_completed`, `account_deletion_result`. Never use identity aliases to merge two established people. |
| **P** — existing + proposed | Existing `onboarding_screen_viewed`, `onboarding_offer_exposed`, `permission_resolved`. Proposed `progress_saved{step_id}`, `progress_resumed{step_id,cause}`, `progress_restore_result{outcome}`, `permission_requested{kind}`, `launch_result{destination,source}`, `startup_recovery{reason_code,outcome}`. Step IDs only; no answers or app selections. |
| **V(name)** — vendor-owned | Adapty forwards actual lifecycle events to PostHog, never fabricated by client checkout: `trial_started`, `trial_converted`, `trial_renewal_cancelled`, `trial_expired`, `subscription_started`, `subscription_renewed`, `subscription_renewal_cancelled`, `subscription_renewal_reactivated`, `subscription_expired`, `subscription_refunded`, `billing_issue_detected`, `entered_grace_period` as applicable. Not every lifecycle produces every event. `access_level_updated` is a **webhook**, not a required PostHog integration event; inspect it through read-only server/vendor evidence. Sharing/inheritance can change access without another `subscription_started`. [Events](https://adapty.io/docs/events), [webhook ordering and fields](https://adapty.io/docs/webhook-event-types-and-fields), [identity sync](https://adapty.io/docs/react-native-identifying-users). |
| **F(reason)** — existing + proposed | Existing PH `subscription_verification_failed{reason_code,stage,recovery,destination}` and SE `SubscriptionVerificationError`. Proposed bounded SE types `CheckoutTechnicalFailure`, `IdentityTechnicalFailure`, `ProgressPersistenceFailure`, `StartupRecoveryFailure`, `DependencyUnavailable`, `EntitlementReconciliationMismatch`; use actual exception types where already present. Include allowlisted operation/stage/code, release/runtime/OTA and short-lived correlation IDs, without raw vendor messages. No SE for expected user actions. |

Names marked **proposed are requirements, not claims about today's telemetry**. The current Sentry scrubber removes contexts, user, general tags and most extras; adding a field at the caller does not make it observable. Verify the final serialized envelope and the actual ingested event. React Native's JavaScript `beforeSend` does not filter native events; audit native data separately. [Current scrubber](/tmp/excer-ref/app-next/src/packages/telemetry/lib/sentry.ts), [Sentry filtering](https://docs.sentry.io/platforms/react-native/configuration/filtering/), [Sentry data scrubbing](https://docs.sentry.io/platforms/react-native/data-management/sensitive-data/).

API requests also need proposed structured **server logs**, independent of PH: route template, random request ID, status, duration, store lane and bounded reason. Log resync start/result and webhook acceptance/reconciliation, including 429/503. Never log authorization headers, bodies, receipts or identifiers. Missing request logs do not prove that no requests occurred.

## Ambiguities that must stay visible

1. Adapty sharing/transfer settings determine how two CUIDs claim one store purchase; current documentation recommends a policy but does not reveal this app's live Production/Sandbox settings. Identifying a user can switch profiles; reload paywalls/products afterward. Do not assume one identify call or matching email performs safe transaction-specific transfer. Read the existing policy and prove I02–I06 on device. [Identity](https://adapty.io/docs/react-native-identifying-users), [sharing](https://adapty.io/docs/sharing-paid-access-between-user-accounts).
2. Package declares `react-native-adapty ^4.0.2`; ADR 0088 analyzes pinned native 4.0.2. Current web docs include newer APIs/4.0.3+ behavior, and this checkout has no generated iOS Podfile.lock. Verify the installed native/JS versions from the signed QA build. Empty restore semantics and Ask to Buy controls cannot be inferred from a floating range.
3. Apple sandbox timings, identity prompts and delivery lag vary. Neither annual conversion/renewal nor every billing/refund branch fits a ten-minute run. Sandbox success does not prove the Production catalog or payment environment; a later authorized Production acceptance check remains separate.
4. Adapty local access protects verified iOS purchases during its outage, but the reference's independent API admission can still reject access. Delivering the rebuild's user-visible outage guarantee requires changing that gate, not merely adding retry events. Offline access beyond available store proof/app allowance is a policy tradeoff; do not confuse it with Apple's configured billing grace.
5. “Never upset” is an acceptance goal, not a technical promise to recover erased anonymous data, override Apple's decline/parental refusal, or extend a confirmed refunded subscription. Explain the available next action and preserve everything the app still owns.

## Critical suite: about ten minutes on one phone

**Purpose:** catch all five reported failure classes—postpurchase UID/admission/429, annual t0 product missing, onboarding restart, Apple sign-in unknown, and invisible checkout/API requests. It cannot guarantee reproduction of an intermittent native/vendor failure or replace the longer matrix.

**Prepare before the clock:** one signed QA phone build with real Apple/Adapty/Supabase calls, runtime/OTA recorded, test inbox signed in, existing eligible/consumed sandbox testers selected as needed, slow enough sandbox term, existing read-only event/log views open, and an already configured local API blocker. Two seeded checkpoints (middle quiz and paywall) can speed setup only if produced through the real app; record their provenance. No dashboard edits, backend restarts, product changes or releases. A clean install and downloading the app are outside the ten-minute budget. If these prerequisites are missing, the suite takes longer; do not count simulated CUID/StoreKit outcomes as live proof.

The phone must be able to reach both the real annual t0 and real trial catalog during this run. If the frozen experiment arm prevents this, use an **already prepared QA-only placement selector** that fetches the existing real placements; it must not stub products or purchase results. Such a selector is not established in the reference. Without one or another existing app route exposing both catalogs, split the catalog checks into two prepared runs and report that the stock-build suite exceeds ten minutes. Switching only the Sandbox Apple Account does not change a frozen Adapty offer arm.
