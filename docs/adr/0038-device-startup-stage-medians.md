# 0038: Device startup stage medians come from a real xctrace capture

## Decision

The device half of the B10/T15 stage-median evidence is captured with
`xcrun xctrace record --instrument os_signpost` against the paired iPhone
(`Il`) and parsed through the existing `scripts/measure-startup.sh
--trace/--xpath` path, now that `scripts/startup-stage-medians.cjs` accepts the
real export shape. Recorded numbers are committed under
`app-next/evidence/T15/device-startup-stage-medians/`. They are
delegate-relative diagnostics on a manual-signed preview Release build:
ADR 0021/0033's boundary is unchanged and the §6.2 budgets remain a signed
distribution build measured on the target device.

## Context

ADR 0033 left the parser's XML shape fixture-verified only, and ADR 0037 added
the simulator unified-log source because no xctrace recording completes against
a simulator on this host (broken CoreSimulator instruments tap). A device was
never the broken half, but the earlier note called it untested because the
paired device was offline. It is not untested now: the same
`--instrument os_signpost` invocation on the device starts, launches, completes
within its time limit and exports the `os-signpost` table normally, with no
`Points of Interest` template (the instrument runs under the implicit Blank
template).

The real export differs from the fixture shape in three ways the parser had to
learn: shared-string definitions (`<process id=… fmt=…>` referenced by
`<process ref=…>`, and likewise `<signpost-name ref=…>`), formatted clocks
(`00:01.172.622` over raw nanosecond text), and a declared signpost name that
must be matched exactly — substring matching reads the legacy
`DelegateToInteractiveHome` interval as the `InteractiveHome` stage. Jest now
covers that shape and the parser records `real_trace_validated: true`.

Two operating facts shape the recording. The device build needs manual signing:
automatic signing fails here (`No Accounts`), and the pre-issued development
profiles it picks lack the associated-domains / push / Sign In with Apple /
family-controls entitlements, while the `ExcerLock Development` and per-extension
development profiles carry them and include the device, so a manual-signed
Release installs and records. The device tap keeps roughly a minute of signpost
data and warms up over the first launches, so one recording must pack its
launches after that warm-up (11 of 20 launches carried the full fresh-install
stage set); the device must also be unlocked, or launches emit no stages.
Alternatives rejected: waiting for a host where the simulator tap works (not
under this project's control), wrapping the numbers as budget acceptance (they
start at the app's AppDelegate entry, not process launch or first frame), and
reading the device's unified log (no bounded device-wide equivalent to
`simctl spawn log show`).

## Consequences

Both lanes now have a real-capture path to the same stage set through one parser
and one JSON schema: the simulator writes them to its unified log (ADR 0037) and
the device to an xctrace export. ADR 0033's "first real capture must confirm the
accepted row shapes" is satisfied, and a parser regression on real exports is
covered by the device-shaped jest fixture instead of only the legacy one.

Costs accepted: device medians depend on the paired hardware being present,
unlocked and dev-signed, so they are evidence rather than a CI gate; the tap
warm-up/retention behavior means a 10-sample loop may need more than ten launch
attempts; `InteractiveHome` still needs the entitled existing-user fixture; and
the build recipe patches the generated `app-next/ios` project per target because
signing is manual here (the patch is local and regenerated by prebuild).
