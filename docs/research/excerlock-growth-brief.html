ExcerLock · The short version

Make the first Unlock real.
Then grow.

People will stay for a Condition that reliably locks their chosen apps and opens them when it is Met—not for a longer quiz or a more dramatic paywall.

Research summary · 27 September 2026 · Recommendations, not measured ExcerLock conversion results

Do these four things first

1

Protect private Activity.

Gym-distance data can enter an analytics event. Remove it and verify what is actually sent. This is a potential privacy path, not proof of a production leak.

2

Make Setup honest.

A Screen Time authorization failure can advance the flow. Keep Setup visibly incomplete until permission and Locked apps are confirmed; offer one clear repair action.

3

Show the real price before purchase.

Make the full annual charge prominent—not only its monthly equivalent. State the trial length, first charge and date, renewal, cancellation, Restore, Terms and Privacy. Only promise a trial reminder if it is actually scheduled.

4

Prove the value on a real iPhone.

Start a trial, select Locked apps, close ExcerLock, see a real Shield, Met a Condition and see the apps Unlock. Repeat after denial, reboot, lapse and Restore. A demo animation or simulator screen cannot prove this.

Then improve the experience

  • Onboarding: get to walking or Gym sooner. Show a clear, editable, achievable first Condition. Label previews as previews. Test a shorter path; do not assume shorter always converts better.
  • Home and Shield: one truthful sentence about whether apps are blocked, why, and what to do next. Give a one-tap repair route when protection is unavailable.
  • Progress and Streak: distinguish “not measured” from “measured but not Met.” Never punish someone for a sensor gap or pretend the phone detected a walk it missed.
  • Settings: make permission repair, notification control, Manage subscription, Restore, export and deletion easy to find. Say plainly that deleting an account does not cancel App Store billing.
  • Voice and visuals: keep the orange-to-ivory, outlined-card identity. Replace “ADDICTED,” brain-trick language and unproven weight or body forecasts with the actual Condition and what it does. Do not add a mascot or a new visual system.

Grow revenue without breaking trust

Keep the existing annual/monthly choices, eligible trial, Restore and win-back path while auditing how they actually work. Do not change prices or trial length because a competitor or industry average seems to earn more. Use win-back only for people eligible under Apple's rules, with the real offer terms visible.

Apple risk: Guideline 4.10 explicitly addresses monetizing Screen Time APIs. The Account Holder and legal counsel need to review what ExcerLock sells before a new monetization push. Competitors being on the App Store is not an exemption. Follow Apple's subscription disclosure guidance; no hidden charges, fake urgency or checkout workaround.

Test in this order

  1. First: a shorter, truthful onboarding and a finishable first Condition—after the real lock and Unlock are reliable.
  2. Next: clearer Setup education and repair, then calm paywall wording. Every paywall version must show the same essential price and cancellation facts.
  3. Later: annual-versus-monthly presentation, eligible win-back and App Store listing creative—only with enough users and approved terms.
Judge success by: a real first lock and Unlock within 72 hours, paid users still active after 7 and 30 days, refunds, cancellation comprehension and support. Do not declare a winner from trial clicks or a tiny sample.

A simple timeline

Now

Fix privacy, Setup and price clarity. Get the Apple policy review. Prove the real iPhone journey.

Next month

Improve first-day success and repair. Run one activation experiment at a time.

After that

Test price presentation and offers only if retention, sample size and policy review support it.
