# Complimentary access for creators

Researched October 13, 2026. This is access without a purchase, keyed by a confirmed account email; it is not a trial or a price experiment.

- [RevenueCat customer profiles: granted entitlements](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/dashboard-and-metrics/customer-profile) documents granting an entitlement directly, removing it, and changing its end date. This establishes a common account entitlement pattern for support and promotional access, separate from paid store subscriptions.
- [Adapty: grant access levels manually](https://adapty.io/docs/grant-access-level) explicitly supports promotional campaigns, investors and support cases without payment. Its server API grants an access level after identifying a customer by `customer_user_id`. This is a viable vendor-owned alternative, but an email recipient who has never registered has no canonical customer ID yet. ExcerLock would still need a pending email grant, audit, revocation and environment rules. Adapty's aggregate profile alone does not establish the store environment required by ADR 0050.
- [Apple: subscription offer codes](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-up-subscription-offer-codes/) supports free or discounted subscription periods, with eligibility rules and redemption through Apple. The current documentation also allows free offers that do not renew. Codes still require a store redemption and a finite subscription offer; they do not provide the requested indefinite, email-only sign-in experience.

Choose a server-owned complimentary grant, resolved from Supabase's confirmed account email in the existing subscription resync. Paid purchase, restore, pricing and attribution remain Adapty-owned. No app-specific claim about Duolingo, Headspace or Superwall's private creator workflows is made: public vendor documentation is sufficient evidence for this decision.

Normalize email by trimming surrounding whitespace and lowercasing, matching the existing email-code adapter. Preserve dots, plus tags and domain spelling. Gmail may deliver variants to one inbox, but rewriting them would grant access to an address the operator did not specify and would behave differently from other providers. Apple Hide My Email must match the relay email actually confirmed on the account.

A grant has no expiry unless an operator supplies one. Date-only `--until` expires at the start of that UTC day. Server responses issue a rolling access boundary at the earlier of the grant expiry and 24 hours from verification. Complimentary remembered admission has no paid three-day grace: expiry is enforced locally even offline. Revocation takes effect on the next successful server check; an offline device can use the last verified grant for at most 24 hours. Research extracts are local evidence in `.excer/research/`; ADR 0077 is the implementation contract.
