# Activity composition provenance

Native recreation of the approved `design/mockups/onboarding-orange/step-5-exercise.png`, requested September 7, 2026. User explicitly requested separate components and library icons. No raster asset was generated.

Icons now render through the canonical registry in `src/icons`. The O03 artwork shows the two activities (walking tile + gym tile), side by side and centred. Walking retains Font Awesome Free 6.x geometry by Fonticons, Inc., CC BY 4.0: https://fontawesome.com/license/free. The dumbbell uses Johan's September 7 rounded reference and is identical to every other gym icon. Independent card transforms remain; icon fills are monochrome. See `src/icons/FOUNDRY.md` and `STYLE.md` for current provenance and geometry rules.

Material Design alternatives and Font Awesome 7.x were inspected but not shipped; the 6.x shapes better match the reference, including the cyclist's open wheels. Local simulator evidence is in `app-next/evidence/O03-reference/`.
