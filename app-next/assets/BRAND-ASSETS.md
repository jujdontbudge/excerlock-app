`google-g.png` is the unmodified 20-point Google G mark in a 24-point crop from the official iOS Light sign-in icon (`iOS/PNG @3x/Light/Theme=Light, Show text=No, Shape=Square, Platform=iOS@3x.png`) in Google's [Sign in with Google assets](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip). The surrounding 44-point button chrome was omitted; the mark remains at its original 20-point size when the crop is displayed at 24 points on a white button.
